Meet the artist

Rooted in
Jackson.

Brian Hunter is a multidisciplinary artist based in Jackson, Mississippi. Moving between painting, drawing, sculpture, and performance, he builds expressive worlds around the figure.

Bold color, enlarged gesture, draped bodies, and unconventional materials give the work its theatrical presence. Across canvas, cloth, and paper, Hunter treats transformation as both a subject and a way of making.
